Unemployment in engineering. Why?

The degree kept its timetable. The job changed. This is the distance between what Indian colleges still examine and what companies hired for this year — by branch, by tier, without the story a senior told you in a corridor.

What moved in 2026

  • AI and ML hiring on Naukri, September: +20%. Hiring of people with 0–3 years: +1%.
  • IT services openings at an 18-month high, and still mostly mid-senior. Entry tech roles were 15,000.
  • 64% of employers called AI, data, or security skills premium no matter the college. 31% still leaned on an IIT or IIM tag.
  • Women’s employability passed men’s, 54% to 51.5%, for the first time in five years of this series.
  • Services firms are cutting the old bulk package and raising the digital one. TCS talked about 25,000 campus hires in FY27, with the higher band growing.

Where the jobs sit

  • IT, software, GCCs33% of engineering jobs

    Still the largest slice, no longer the default destination of every branch. Inside it, entry-level tech openings were 15,000 of 1.23 lakh in October 2026.

  • Manufacturing and industrial15%

    The second-largest destination. Invisible in most placement-week conversations, which only count companies that sit in an auditorium.

  • Construction and real estate13%

    Larger than the civil placement rate suggests, because a lot of it never becomes a campus offer.

  • Electronics, semiconductors, auto and EV, energy26% together

    The bloc that did not exist as a campus story a decade ago. It does not have room for every student who was told to “learn coding instead”.

Do not treat these as the same number

Employability. Share of test-takers above a cut-off. A person can be employable and unemployed.

Campus placement. Offers divided by a cohort the college counted. It misses off-campus jobs and higher study, and it misses people the cell never registered.

Candidates per job. Active profiles against active postings on one platform in August 2026. A market snapshot, not the labour force.

The PDF is not the college

Tier here is a hiring-market signal, not a judgement of intellect. The gap index you are browsing uses Tier 3. Two colleges on one affiliating university can share a syllabus and not share a lab.

Tier 1

IITs, IISc, IIIT Hyderabad, BITS Pilani, and the old NITs that still run real electives. The PDF is mostly taught. The gap that remains is what no Indian syllabus examines yet: shipped AI systems, release-quality drawings, silicon that actually simulates.

Tier 2

Newer NITs and IIITs, DTU, NSUT, Jadavpur, strong state colleges, and the better deemed universities. Scheme is often current. Labs and internships are uneven, so about half the written skill survives contact with a recruiter.

Tier 3

The majority: private colleges on AKTU, VTU, JNTU, Anna, Mumbai, GTU, MAKAUT, RGPV and the rest. Same university PDF as a stronger campus, a fraction of the practice. This is the default, because it is where most students actually sit.

VTU

The 2022 scheme still governs students admitted then. The 2025 scheme adds AI and computational work across branches, including mechanical, civil, and electrical, but only from that admission year.

AKTU

The 2025–26 final-year CSE papers name AI, IoT, cloud, and cryptography, plus MOOCs. A named elective is not a deployment, and most of the university’s students are not in a lab that can support one.

JNTU, Anna, Mumbai, SPPU, GTU, MAKAUT, RGPV

One PDF, many colleges. The gap this report measures is not only old topics. It is the distance between the PDF and the Tuesday lab.

IITs (the 23)

The tag still opens doors. Employers in the 2026 skills report already say an AI skill can outweigh it. The syllabus is not the advantage. The peers, labs, and internships are.

CSE

Four moves. Not a new personality.

The dangerous advice is not lazy. It is last year’s advice, repeated by someone who was hired under last year’s requisition. Keep what still works. Drop what only worked because companies were buying headcount.

  1. 01

    Keep DSA. Stop pretending it is the whole job. Add one tested service: auth, a database with real queries, and a readme a stranger can run.

  2. 02

    Put Git, Linux, and a test suite on that service. These are still missing from most papers and visible in almost every posting.

  3. 03

    Build one small AI feature you can defend: retrieval over your own notes, an evaluation set, a failure case. Not a chatbot screenshot.

  4. 04

    Apply where the work matches the build — GCCs, product teams, the digital track of services — not only the company your senior joined in 2019.
